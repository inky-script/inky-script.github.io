// how this site was built

Built with AI, checked by me

I study data, not front-end. Instead of spending weeks learning a framework, I used AI as a coding assistant and kept the thinking, decisions and verification for myself. It's the same way I'd use AI as an analyst.

01 · DEFINE

Write the requirements

Goal, audience (recruiters), sections, content rules, constraints: one HTML file, no frameworks, mobile-first.

02 · GENERATE

Prompt the AI

Gave it clear specs and my real project reports as context, then iterated section by section.

03 · VALIDATE

Review & test

Checked every fact against my reports, tested on phone and desktop, light and dark mode, and fixed bugs myself.

04 · PROTECT

Keep data safe

Only shared non-confidential, public information with AI tools. Personal and client data stays out of prompts.
